Privacy

This site is run by the Running Hackathon team. You can reach us at runninghackathon@gmail.com.

What this site collects

Nothing. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, has no forms and stores nothing in your browser.

If you email us

We keep your name, email address and message so that we can reply and arrange sponsorship or a place at an event. Our mailbox is provided by Google. Ask us at any time and we will delete your emails.

Who else is involved

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs the IP addresses of visitors. The London film is served by Vercel when you press play. The sponsor buttons open a draft in Gmail, or in your own mail app on a phone.

Photographs and film

If you appear in a photograph or film on this site and would like it taken down, email us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
