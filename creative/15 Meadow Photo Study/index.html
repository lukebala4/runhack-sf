What feels more photographic?

Varied focus
Sharp details give way to softer ones. Skin, hair and distant foliage do not all need the same level of definition.
Warm light, cooler shadows
The sunlight can feel warm while the shaded areas stay neutral or cool. A single golden wash makes the scene feel processed.
Irregular natural detail
Uneven grass, stray hairs and broken tree edges feel more convincing than uniformly smooth or repeated textures.
