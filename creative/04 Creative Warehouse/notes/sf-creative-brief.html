A useful first creative brief

Make the run and the build feel part of the same day. Photograph people moving together, then their messy desks, screens, drinks and finished ideas. Let the work and personalities carry the tone.

Start visual exploration with a track-blue or court-green accent, warm paper, dark type and natural photography. Test a simple athletic display face against one expressive serif or handwritten detail. The board contains several different type treatments; it does not yet prescribe a single font system.

For a first poster or launch page, try one strong action image, Running Hackathon SF, the practical event information once confirmed, and a small irreverent detail. For social posts, keep room for founder humour and in-progress work alongside sports images.

Possible outputs to develop next

1. A short event brief covering date, audience, capacity, format, budget and success criteria.
2. A San Francisco venue and run-route shortlist, researched once those constraints are known.
3. Two visual directions using the reference archive: an athletic treatment and a more playful builder-culture treatment.
4. Original event photography and graphics developed from these themes, with specific images cleared before external reuse.
