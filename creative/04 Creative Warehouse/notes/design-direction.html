Running Hackathon SF: morning studies

## Subject and purpose
Local-only website concepts for a running hackathon. The job of the hero is to make running and building feel like one good day with interesting people. SF means San Francisco; dates, venue and registration are not confirmed. The user explicitly requested working localhost concepts; this authorizes implementation of reviewable prototypes. This is a fresh standalone study, not an edit to either existing website repository.

## Two directions
Track morning: compact paper navigation; expressive but simple headline next to short copy; large horizontal runner photograph; a loose runner sketch lower on the page. Warmth comes from the russet track and sun. Signature is the full-stride image with brick-orange shoes.

Midsummer fields: a full photographic scene, modest type positioned in the pale sky, friends running through grass, lots of breathing room and a few observational ink marks. Signature is a small, lived-in running crew in a large morning landscape.

## Structure and behaviour
The local studio has a separate concept switcher, a live comparison view and notes. Product navigation anchors to real sections. Each concept includes hero, idea/format, and an SF coming-soon section. No forms collecting data or invented event dates. Comparison previews preserve desktop composition and scale to their containers. All imagery served locally. Keyboard focus, no horizontal overflow, semantic HTML, descriptive alt text, reduced-motion support.

## Motion
Subtle entry or line gestures at most. No parallax or continuous marquee. Rive is optional if a real interactive/skeletal animation earns its cost; static artwork should not require a runtime.

## Methods considered
1. Edit the production repository: strongest route to shipping later, but confounds exploration with the old design and adds deployment risk. Not selected for this local review.
2. Canvas/mockup only: quick visual divergence but cannot establish responsive behaviour, real image loading or interaction. Superdesign reference extraction is useful; static mockups alone do not meet the localhost request.
3. Independent buildless local concept studio: no dependency install, no deployment or licensing purchase, real assets and responsive HTML, easy to compare and reuse. Selected as the least complex route to the user's requested review.

## Five merged iterations, 16 October
The next iteration must literally combine the track and meadow in every photographic scene. Five newly generated images use both original references. Keep all original images and independent dated archive copies intact. These are imagined scenes, not SF event photography.

The thesis is warm track energy spilling into an open midsummer landscape. Retain paper and ink; all saturated colour comes from the photograph. Strong 80–132px desktop headlines, 48–72px mobile, 18–22px body. No decorative microcopy or eyebrow slogans. Each version is a complete responsive page:

Chosen main headline: **Good ideas need fresh air**, across all five new concepts. Show it as a large statement, with responsive line breaks; keep both archived original concepts intact.

1. Open track: large paper headline above a panoramic, lateral running photo. Editorial two-column introduction, clean baseline.
2. Meadow lane: type across pale sky above a curving track through the field. Image-led, long view, generous photographic height.
3. Morning club: asymmetric 40/60 paper-and-photo split, close running crew, large stacked proposition. Body copy sits with the headline, not over athletes. Stacks below 1000px to preserve the headline.
4. Grass and lanes: broad photograph first, followed immediately by an oversized statement on paper. Low visual noise and confident image priority.
5. Out and back: immersive low rear perspective, large headline in the open left sky, nearest runner on the right. Full scene retains track, grass and footwear.

The comparison uses two readable columns, then a centered fifth card. A functional concept picker opens all five and the two originals. No five-column miniature layout. Reduced motion and static previews avoid unnecessary runtimes; the existing authored Rive runner is reserved for a below-fold full-page accent.
