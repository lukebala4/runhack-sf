Use case: logo-brand mascot concept, single original character illustration for Running Hackathon.
Create one cool, techy running sloth with an understated, slightly scruffy personality. He is a curious builder out for an early morning run: relaxed, amused, quietly confident and alert. Friendly to first-time runners. A recognizable sloth first, even without clothing: warm taupe bean-shaped torso, small cream oval face with dark sloth eye patches, one raised brow, asymmetric half-smile, long loose swinging arms and three short blunt curved fingers. Adult cartoon proportions, small head compared to the body, not a baby animal. An easy jogging stride with one foot lifted, clearly running but not an elite sprint pose. Long arms and negative space around them give an unmistakable silhouette.
Wear a faded forest-green running singlet, cream track shorts and chunky brick-orange retro running shoes. Slim dark sunglasses are pushed up on the head so the face and expression stay visible. A single small wrist device is the subtle tech detail. No laptop or other handheld props. Real-life warm muted colours, no neon.
Style: confident, simple flat editorial cartoon, dark cocoa slightly uneven ink outlines, restrained soft screenprint texture, broad flat colour shapes, very little shading. Approachable independent running-club sticker character. Readable at 160px tall. No individually rendered fur, no glossy eyes, no 3D, no gradients or rim glow, no oversized cute head. Original anatomy and face; do not copy an otter, hedgehog or existing company mascot.
Composition: full body, entire arms, fingers and both shoes visible, centred with generous transparent margin, one character only, no scene, ground shadow, border, text, letters or logos. Genuine transparent RGBA background.
