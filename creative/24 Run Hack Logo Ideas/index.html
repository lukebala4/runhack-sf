Run Hacktwenty logo ideas.

A new, more underground look for the Running Hackathon. There are emblems, seals, stamps and wordmarks. Some spell out the full name and some just say Run Hack.

Every idea takes its colour, texture and type from the ideas-doc mood board below. Click a logo to see it bigger. Star the ones you like; your stars are saved in this browser.

Style guide

  • — Motion blur and film grain
  • — Wide caps spaced out, stacked stamps
  • — Cut-out serifs and soft, rounded letters
  • — Sparkles, sun blobs, little runners
